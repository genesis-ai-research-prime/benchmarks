# Benchmarks and Certification

## Proof levels

Genesis proof is staged, in three levels. The levels are not equivalent, and a pass at one level certifies that level only.

- **Level A — Fabric and flow control.** The Governor seated and acting on the fabric: flows observed and controlled at the execution layer.
- **Level B — Request-scoped intervention.** Intervention scoped to individual requests: a run that leaves its envelope is acted on, at the level of the request that left it.
- **Level C — Anomaly and coherence recognition.** Recognition of the run's own state — including validation against the coherence band. Coherence-band validation applies only at Level C.

## Rules of certification

- Metrics and PASS/FAIL criteria are defined according to the selected proof level (A, B or C), agreed before the benchmark runs.
- Certification applies only to the agreed proof level — never automatically to broader commercial deployment.
- A benchmark certified in one territory is evidence in every territory: no territory pays twice to prove the same fact.
- The method is never recorded here. This room holds outcomes: the level, the territory, the hardware seat, the date, and the result.
